Why we exist

Local context. Real work.

Gusii Digital was born in Kisii. We understand the unique challenges and opportunities of businesses here, in Nyamira and across the wider Gusii region. Whether you run a shop in town, a farm, a school, a hotel or a professional service, we help you build a strong online presence that brings real customers.

We don’t start with jargon or a template. We start with your business, your customers and how they find you — then build the simplest thing that works. If something isn’t worth your money, we’ll tell you.

As social media has grown, so has a new question: can attention become income? We help creators and businesses answer that honestly, with a plan instead of a promise.

Our mark

A highland sunrise, a way forward.

The Gusii Digital mark holds the highlands in a leaf-shaped ring: terraced hills, a gold sun and birds above, with an orange arrow growing out and upward. “Gusii” is set in deep forest green; “Digital” in terracotta.

It is a quiet nod to where we are from — and a clear signal of where we want your business to go. The emblem works on WhatsApp, Google profiles and business cards; the full lockup is used here and on documents.
