Questions

Good to know.

Still unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp and we’ll give you a straight answer.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what the site needs to do — the number of pages, photos, bookings and extras. After a short conversation we send a clear fixed quote, so you know the full cost before we start. No hidden extras.

Can you help me make money from social media?

Sometimes, and not overnight. We look at your audience, the offers you can realistically fulfil, and which pathways fit — direct sales, brand deals, referrals or paid content. We build the content, offer and tracking, but we don’t sell guaranteed income. If monetisation isn’t realistic yet, we’ll say so and focus on audience or enquiries first.

Do I need thousands of followers before monetising?

No. A focused, engaged audience can be more valuable than a large passive one. For many local businesses, the first win is direct sales or a simple referral arrangement, not a brand deal.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I still need a website?

Facebook is great for conversation and updates. A website gives you one official link you control, with your services, location, photos and a clear way to enquire. We can connect the two so they work together.

Do you only work with businesses in Kisii?

We’re based in Kisii and love working face-to-face across the Gusii region. We also work remotely with clients elsewhere in Kenya — WhatsApp, calls and screen sharing make it straightforward.

I’m not good with technology. Will I manage?

That’s normal, and it’s our job to make it simple. We set things up, keep the tools practical, and show you how to check enquiries. You can always reach us on WhatsApp afterwards.

Can you help a small duka or a new business?

Yes. Some of the most useful work is getting a small business found on Google and reachable on WhatsApp. We’ll start with what makes sense for your stage and your budget.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us the problem in plain words. We’ll suggest the smallest thing that can help — and tell you if it isn’t worth doing yet.
